1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraScan ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, handles, and safeguards your information when you use our Android mobile application. AuraScan complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data policy and Permissions requirements.

By downloading, installing, or using AuraScan, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Permissions Requested & Why We Need Them

AuraScan adheres to the principle of least privilege, requesting only standard and runtime permissions essential to app functionality:

Permission Type Purpose & Usage
CAMERA Runtime Required solely to allow you to take photos of paper documents, bills, IDs, and notes within the app viewfinder. We never access your camera in the background or without your explicit action.
INTERNET Standard Used to securely connect to the Google Gemini AI API for optical character recognition (OCR), document classification, and summary generation, and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
VIBRATE Standard Provides subtle haptic feedback when you successfully capture a page or tap action buttons for an improved user experience.

3. Information We Collect and Process

A. User-Provided Content (Local Scans & Images)

  • Document Images & Pages: Photos you capture with the camera or import from your photo picker are saved locally in the app's sandboxed internal storage on your device.
  • Extracted Text & Expense Records: Text extracted from documents and bill entries (merchant, amount, GST) are stored securely in your on-device local database.

B. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Document Analysis

When you choose to analyze a document, the document image is sent via secure, encrypted HTTPS transmission to the Google Gemini API to perform text recognition (OCR), auto-classify the document type (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN card, Invoice, Textbook Notes, Legal), and provide summaries.

  • We do not store your documents on private remote servers.
  • Your documents and personal text are not used to train machine learning models.
  • Data transmission is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

C. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

AuraScan uses Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable device data in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policies:

  • Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device identifiers, device model, and operating system version
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention)
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

For more details on how Google uses data from apps using its services, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information processed by AuraScan is used exclusively to:

  • Capture, crop, filter, and organize your scanned documents.
  • Extract OCR text and generate formatted PDF documents and images for export.
  • Calculate bill totals, split shares, and log personal expenses locally.
  • Provide rewarded app features through Google AdMob.

5. Data Retention, Storage, and Deletion

  • Local Storage: All scanned files, images, PDFs, and expense records remain on your device.
  • User-Controlled Deletion: You have full control over your data. Deleting a scan or an expense entry in the app immediately and permanently purges it from your device storage.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored scans, cached thumbnails, and local database records via standard Android sandboxing.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AuraScan is a general audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.

8. Security Safeguards

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures:

  • All network transmissions (API calls and ad requests) are strictly forced over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Local app files are kept in Android private app storage, inaccessible to other apps without root permissions.
  • No user accounts, passwords, or personal credentials are required to use AuraScan.
